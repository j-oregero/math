Currently, I'm an Assistant Professor at the University of Texas, Rio Grande Valley. Prior to this I held an Adams Visiting Assistant Professor position at the University of Kansas in Lawrence, KS as well as postdoctoral positions at the University of Central Florida in Orlando, FL, and at the Mathematical Sciences Research Institute in Berkeley, CA (now the Simons Laufer Mathematical Sciences Institute). In 2021 I received my PhD in Mathematics from University at Buffalo, SUNY under the supervision of Professor Gino Biondini. My research is broadly concerned with th analysis of partial differential equations (PDEs) arising in mathematical physics with a particular interest in nonlinear dispersive equations, integrable systems, spectral and inverse spectral theory, singular asymptotics, and stability. Recently, I've been studying problems related to (i) modulational instability (or Benjamin-Feir instability) of periodic wave trains, (ii) long-time asymptotic analysis of integrable equations and their non-integrable perturbations, and (iii) the description of random nonlinear waves in physical systems modeled by soliton gases.
